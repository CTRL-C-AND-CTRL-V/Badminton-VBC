About the Centre

A home for the community, built for the game.

Victoria Badminton Centre is Melbourne's most ambitious badminton facility — a purpose-built venue designed around one simple belief: great badminton deserves great space.

From families picking up a racquet for the first time to elite players training for national competition, our 36 courts across two sports — 28 badminton (including 2 SVIP) and 8 pickleball — professional lighting, and sprung sports flooring create the conditions for your best game.

Pro Lighting
Shadow-free LED rigs tuned for shuttlecock clarity.
Sprung Flooring
BWF-approved surfaces for performance and joint care.

Getting Here

Parking & transport

Free on-site parking with over 120 spaces, a direct train line, and just 15 minutes from the city.

120+

Free On-Site Parking

No fees · No permits

Over 120 dedicated spaces right at the centre, including accessible bays near the main entrance. Always free for players and visitors — just park and play.

Direct Train Access

500m from Tottenham Station

Tottenham Station is just a 500-metre walk away — roughly six minutes on foot — putting the centre within easy reach by train, no transfers needed.

15 Minutes From the City

By car

An easy 15-minute drive from Melbourne's CBD, with quick access from the West Gate Freeway and Sunshine Road.

Peak evenings and weekends are our busiest times — allow a few extra minutes before your booking, or take the train from Tottenham Station.
